汉字 Hanzi Swap Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Hanzi Swap is a browser extension that helps you learn Chinese by replacing English words on the web pages you visit with Chinese words. This policy explains what the extension does with your data. The short version: everything stays on your device. We don't collect, sell or share anything.

What the extension reads

To swap words, the extension reads the text of the web pages you open. This happens entirely inside your browser. Page text is never sent anywhere, stored, or logged.

What the extension stores, and where

The extension saves the following in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your own device:

  • Your learning progress: which words are unlocked, introduced, answered correctly or incorrectly, and mastered.
  • Words you add yourself with right-click → “Add to Hanzi Swap” (the Chinese, pinyin and English you enter).
  • Your settings, such as whether swapping is on and any sites where you paused it.

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the extension deletes it, and “Reset progress” in the extension's menu clears your progress.

Translation

When you add a word that isn't in the built-in vocabulary, the extension may suggest a translation using Chrome's built-in translator, which runs on your device. The word is not sent to us or to any other service by the extension.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ins, analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to third parties.
  • No data is sold or used for anything other than the extension's learning features.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if paid features are added later, the updated policy will be posted at this address with a new date before the change takes effect.
